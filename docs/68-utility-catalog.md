# Utility Catalog

## Catalog status

This is the accepted content-complete utility catalog for initial playtesting. DEC-109 fixes the catalog at twelve non-radar utilities, two per specialized material, plus the universal common-ore resource radar. [DEC-116](./decisions/DEC-116-accept-initial-utility-catalog.md) accepts the concepts, assignments, interactions, first-playable subset, and numeric starting values below. [DEC-121](./decisions/DEC-121-accept-initial-option-unlock-catalog.md) fixes which utilities begin unlocked and how the other six enter the account catalog.

## Design goals

- Utilities support the mech, mining loop, exploration, economy, survivability, mobility, or equipped weapons without becoming additional automatic weapons.
- Each material offers one broadly understandable choice and one choice with a more situational build or routing role.
- Every four-material profile offers a credible mixture of offense, defense, mobility, recovery, mining, economy, and exploration, but profiles are allowed to be lopsided.
- No utility is required for baseline completion, and no three-item set is intended to be correct for every mech, profile, and route.
- Utilities add no manual activation, aiming, placement, or targeting input.
- Effects are deterministic and fully previewed before the irreversible slot commitment.

## Shared acquisition and rank rules

- Every non-radar utility costs one unit of its assigned specialized material and permanently fills one of three utility slots.
- Each begins at its listed **Installed** effect and has three common-ore ranks costing 50, 100, and 150 ore.
- The four values shown for a utility are its Installed, Rank 1, Rank 2, and Rank 3 totals—not cumulative additions.
- A utility is unique within the loadout and cannot be fabricated twice.
- Utilities have no branches, alternative recipes, replacement, dismantling, sale, or refund during a run.
- Fabrication previews all four effect tiers, the current build's affected systems, and any equipped weapon that receives no benefit from a weapon-support utility.
- The resource radar remains outside the material table: it costs 300 common ore, has no ranks, and retains its accepted seven-category directional behavior.

## Asterite utilities

### UTL-A1 — Harmonic Calibrator

The calibrator aligns every equipped weapon's damage output.

| State | Weapon damage |
| --- | ---: |
| Installed | +8% |
| Rank 1 | +12% |
| Rank 2 | +16% |
| Rank 3 | +20% |

- Applies to damage attributed to any equipped weapon, including that weapon's mines, drones, turrets, persistent zones, delayed attacks, and selected branch behavior.
- Does not increase enemy damage, contact effects generated by the mech itself unless they belong to a weapon, environmental damage, mining resonance, or temporary effects that are not weapon attacks.
- Its universal applicability makes it reliable, while its slot and full investment of one specialized unit plus 300 common ore compete with more specialized utility effects and weapon development.

### UTL-A2 — Survey Aperture

The aperture extends the mech's ordinary visual and sensor discovery radius.

| State | Discovery radius |
| --- | ---: |
| Installed | +20% |
| Rank 1 | +30% |
| Rank 2 | +40% |
| Rank 3 | +50% |

- Applies to fog reveal and normal discovery of terrain, routes, landmarks, mining points, relic caches, and destructible rocks.
- A newly discovered object's ordinary persistent map marker appears under its normal rules.
- Uses the same occlusion and reveal rules as normal exploration; it changes radius rather than granting omniscience through blocked terrain.
- Does not provide directions, exact distances, hidden map markers, or a replacement for the resource radar.
- Does not alter boss-loot markers, which already appear immediately.

## Barysteel utilities

### UTL-B1 — Reinforced Bulkhead

The bulkhead increases maximum Hull Integrity while preserving the mech's current missing-Hull amount.

| State | Maximum Hull Integrity |
| --- | ---: |
| Installed | +15 |
| Rank 1 | +25 |
| Rank 2 | +35 |
| Rank 3 | +45 |

- Fabricating or ranking the bulkhead increases current and maximum Hull Integrity by the same amount. A mech missing 20 Hull before purchase remains 20 below its new maximum afterward.
- The increase combines with account and mech maximum-Hull modifiers and is shown in the fabrication preview.
- It supplies capacity rather than damage reduction or passive repair, keeping it distinct from Armor and Recovery.

### UTL-B2 — Extraction Accelerator

The accelerator modestly increases forward mining extraction speed.

| State | Forward extraction rate |
| --- | ---: |
| Installed | +5% |
| Rank 1 | +10% |
| Rank 2 | +15% |
| Rank 3 | +20% |

- Applies to standard seams, rich seams, material geodes, and Hyper Gold sites.
- A +20% extraction rate completes a nominal 15-second seam in 12.5 seconds, a 20-second geode in about 16.7 seconds, and a 45-second Hyper Gold site in 37.5 seconds before interruptions.
- Mining decay remains four times the mech's current forward extraction rate. The utility shortens successful holds without improving the fraction of progress retained after leaving.
- Does not change payouts, completed checkpoints, the 0.5-second leaving grace period, threat-beacon progress thresholds, or enemies already summoned.

## Cinderglass utilities

### UTL-C1 — Cycle Capacitor

The capacitor increases the activation frequency of automatic weapons.

| State | Weapon attack rate |
| --- | ---: |
| Installed | +8% |
| Rank 1 | +12% |
| Rank 2 | +16% |
| Rank 3 | +20% |

- Increases attacks per second rather than subtracting the displayed percentage from cooldown.
- Applies to each weapon's primary repeat or activation schedule, including autonomous weapon attacks where that schedule exists.
- Does not accelerate damage ticks inside an already-created effect, delayed secondary pulses, mine arming, projectile travel, drone movement, or non-weapon timers unless a weapon specification explicitly identifies that timing as its attack rate.
- Fabrication lists which equipped weapons and branch behaviors receive the modifier.

### UTL-C2 — Capacitor Screen

The screen stores one automatic charge that negates the next incoming damage instance.

| State | Recharge time | Stored charges |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Installed | 24s | 1 |
| Rank 1 | 21s | 1 |
| Rank 2 | 18s | 1 |
| Rank 3 | 15s | 1 |

- The utility begins charged when fabricated. When charged, it completely negates the first contact, projectile, or hazard damage instance that would otherwise remove Hull Integrity, then begins recharging.
- It holds at most one charge. Ranking it shortens recharge but does not immediately restore a missing charge.
- The recharge timer advances only during active gameplay simulation and freezes during fabrication, pause, relic resolution, and other complete-simulation pauses.
- A negated hit causes no Hull loss and does not trigger effects that require damage taken. Further simultaneous or later hits apply normally after the charge is consumed.
- A clear shield ring and utility-slot charge indicator communicate ready, consumed, and recharging states without requiring player input.

## Driftmetal utilities

### UTL-D1 — Vector Thrusters

The thrusters permanently increase movement speed for the remainder of the run.

| State | Movement speed |
| --- | ---: |
| Installed | +6% |
| Rank 1 | +9% |
| Rank 2 | +12% |
| Rank 3 | +15% |

- Applies to direct movement in every direction without changing acceleration, stopping, facing, collision, mining progress, or enemy speed.
- Combines with mech, PowerUp, relic, and temporary movement modifiers and appears in the displayed final movement statistic.

### UTL-D2 — Extraction Tether

The tether increases the radius within which the mech automatically mines.

| State | Extraction-zone radius |
| --- | ---: |
| Installed | +10% |
| Rank 1 | +15% |
| Rank 2 | +20% |
| Rank 3 | +25% |

- Applies to standard seams, rich seams, material geodes, and Hyper Gold sites.
- The visible extraction circle expands to match the effective interaction boundary; activation, grace, and decay use that displayed boundary.
- Does not increase extraction speed, payout, resonance-field size, threat-beacon reach, or resource discovery radius.
- Material-geode resonance fields remain larger than the maximum expanded extraction zone, preserving their danger role.

## Eidolon Coral utilities

### UTL-E1 — Repair Swarm

The swarm supplies continuous passive Recovery.

| State | Recovery |
| --- | ---: |
| Installed | +0.10 Hull/s |
| Rank 1 | +0.15 Hull/s |
| Rank 2 | +0.20 Hull/s |
| Rank 3 | +0.25 Hull/s |

- Adds to other Recovery sources and functions during movement, combat, and mining.
- Cannot raise current Hull Integrity above its maximum.
- Recovery and its timers freeze whenever the complete gameplay simulation is paused.
- The active HUD reports Recovery without requiring a constant large healing effect; periodic repair ticks receive a restrained visible and audible cue.

### UTL-E2 — Priority Uplink

The uplink coordinates all equipped weapons against elites and bosses.

| State | Weapon damage to elites and bosses |
| --- | ---: |
| Installed | +15% |
| Rank 1 | +25% |
| Rank 2 | +35% |
| Rank 3 | +45% |

- Applies to damage attributed to every equipped weapon when the damaged enemy is explicitly classified as an elite or boss.
- Does not increase damage against ordinary enemies, wave-event formations, destructible rocks, mining objects, or other targets merely because they have high Hull or appear late in the run.
- Threat-beacon enemies receive the bonus only when the encounter explicitly uses an elite or boss identity.
- The target's elite or boss classification and the resulting bonus are visible in damage breakdowns and expanded combat statistics.

## Flux Amber utilities

### UTL-F1 — Ore Catalyzer

The catalyzer increases common basic ore obtained directly through mining.

| State | Mined common ore |
| --- | ---: |
| Installed | +10% |
| Rank 1 | +15% |
| Rank 2 | +20% |
| Rank 3 | +25% |

- Applies to common ore installments from standard and rich seams and to the common-ore jackpot from material geodes.
- Does not duplicate specialized-material units or Hyper Gold and does not modify boss loot, relic sales, refunds, or other non-mining awards.
- Fractional bonus ore carries forward and awards one whole ore whenever the accumulated fraction reaches one. The expanded utility detail shows the carried fraction; it does not require another permanent active-HUD meter. An uncompleted fraction has no spendable value and ends with the run.
- Existing mined ore is never increased retroactively.

### UTL-F2 — Field Expander

The expander increases scalable spatial dimensions of weapon attacks.

| State | Weapon area |
| --- | ---: |
| Installed | +8% |
| Rank 1 | +12% |
| Rank 2 | +16% |
| Rank 3 | +20% |

- Applies to weapon-authored scalable radii, diameters, widths, blast areas, projectile bodies, cones, and persistent damage zones.
- Does not change targeting or placement range, projectile travel distance, orbit radius, mining zones, discovery radius, pickup radius, or resonance fields unless a weapon specification explicitly treats that dimension as Area.
- Fabrication lists the dimensions affected on every equipped weapon and warns when an equipped behavior receives no benefit.

## Modifier and timing rules

- Flat statistic changes apply before percentage modifiers. Percentage modifiers to the same named statistic add together, and the displayed final result is authoritative.
- Attack rate is represented as attacks per second or equivalent frequency; a +20% attack-rate total produces 1.2 times the unmodified activation frequency.
- Recovery sources add in Hull Integrity per second.
- Already-created finite projectiles, zones, mines, and similar weapon instances retain the damage, geometry, and lifetime with which they were created. New instances use the newly fabricated or ranked utility values.
- Persistent autonomous units remain in place and use new utility values for attacks they perform after play resumes.
- Fabrication and utility ranking occur during a complete-simulation pause, so no duration, cooldown, Recovery, or mining state advances while the purchase is reviewed.
- If a later relic explicitly replaces a statistic or rule, its description must state whether it overrides or combines with these utilities. Silent exceptions are not allowed.

## Fresh-profile and unlocked availability

The fresh profile begins with one utility per material:

| Material | Fresh-profile utility | Coverage role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asterite | Harmonic Calibrator | Direct offense |
| Barysteel | Extraction Accelerator | Mining speed |
| Cinderglass | Cycle Capacitor | Weapon tempo |
| Driftmetal | Vector Thrusters | Mobility |
| Eidolon Coral | Repair Swarm | Recovery |
| Flux Amber | Ore Catalyzer | Economy |

Every four-material profile initially offers exactly four of these plus the resource radar. Buying the 600-Hyper-Gold **Advanced Utility Suite** permanently unlocks Survey Aperture, Reinforced Bulkhead, Capacitor Screen, Extraction Tether, Priority Uplink, and Field Expander together. Every subsequent four-material profile then offers eight non-radar utilities plus the radar.

The purchase adds blueprints only. An unlocked utility still requires its assigned material in the current profile, costs one unit to fabricate, fills one of three utility slots, and uses the listed common-ore ranks. See the [Permanent Option-Unlock Catalog](./63-permanent-option-unlock-catalog.md).

## Balance risks to test

- Whether Harmonic Calibrator or Cycle Capacitor becomes an automatic choice whenever present.
- Whether Extraction Accelerator or Extraction Tether removes too much mining commitment when combined with mining PowerUps or mech traits.
- Whether Vector Thrusters dominates because movement improves combat, travel, and mining avoidance simultaneously.
- Whether Capacitor Screen trivializes isolated boss projectiles while contributing too little against dense contact pressure.
- Whether Repair Swarm invalidates 25-Hull health packs or becomes ineffective under the accepted late-run damage cadence and four-to-eight-pack natural-run target.
- Whether Ore Catalyzer repays its slot and ore ranks too quickly or too slowly.
- Whether Survey Aperture feels meaningfully different from the resource radar at the fixed wide camera scale.
- Whether Priority Uplink becomes automatic for boss-focused builds or remains too narrow during ordinary waves.
- Whether Field Expander benefits enough of the fifteen-weapon catalog to justify an irreversible slot.
- Whether any four-material profile lacks a plausible utility trio for multiple mech and weapon archetypes.
