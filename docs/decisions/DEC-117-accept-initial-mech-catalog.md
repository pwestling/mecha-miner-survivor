# DEC-117 — Accept the Initial Mech Catalog

## Decision

Accept the six identities, signature pairings, simple inherent traits, top-down silhouette requirements, initial availability, and selection rules in the [Initial Mech Catalog](../36-initial-mech-catalog.md).

| Mech | Signature | Trait |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kestrel | Pulse Repeater | +15% attack rate |
| Pike | Rail Lance | +15% weapon damage |
| Prospector | Missile Rack | +15% mining extraction rate |
| Lodestar | Gravity Projector | +15% weapon area |
| Bastion | Reactor Pulse | +25 maximum Hull Integrity |
| Razorback | Ram Field | +10% movement speed |

All six initial mechs are available on a fresh profile. Kestrel is the recommended and initially focused option rather than a forced selection. Later roster additions may use banked Hyper Gold or extraction-secured challenges, but failed or abandoned runs never permanently unlock a mech.

## Status

Accepted as the initial mech-content and selection baseline. Exact numeric balance and final presentation naming remain playtest and production work.

## Rationale

Six simple positive specializations reproduce the approachable character-selection role of the genre reference without importing XP scaling or creating geology-dependent characters. Each trait is immediately demonstrated by its signature or the universal mining loop, remains useful after later fabrication, and stacks legibly with one accepted utility.

The silhouettes deliberately cover six top-down shape families that can be produced through low-poly asset adaptation. Making the initial six available immediately supports signature and profile experimentation, avoids forcing one chassis until a first 35-minute success, and preserves the survival-gated rule for any later cross-run roster progression.

## Consequences

- OQ-029 is resolved except for final presentation names and numeric playtesting.
- The selection interface gains a concrete roster, comparison statistics, locked-entry presentation, and Random Mech behavior.
- The first-run interface recommends Kestrel and Pulse Repeater while allowing any initial mech.
- Content production requires six silhouettes despite the current dedicated free-mech pack containing four models.
- Permanent PowerUp design must evaluate additive stacking with all six traits.
- Later roster additions require their own survival-gated unlock decisions; this catalog creates no failed-run progression exception.
