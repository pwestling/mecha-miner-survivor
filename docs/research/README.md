# Research Index

Research notes support design decisions but do not become canonical gameplay rules merely by being recorded.

## Research note requirements

Each `RES-###-topic.md` note records:

- The design question being researched.
- The retrieval or observation date.
- Sources with direct links and enough context to verify them.
- Relevant facts separated from interpretation.
- Comparisons that use consistent dimensions.
- Inferences, uncertainties, and source limitations.
- Possible implications for this game, clearly labeled as proposals.
- Links to resulting open questions, decisions, and specification sections.

Prefer primary and authoritative sources. When studying another game, distinguish official descriptions from direct observation, community documentation, and subjective player commentary.
